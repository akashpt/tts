Overview

Real-Time Defect Detection for Spinning

Real-time defect detection for spinning processes — automatically identifies Lycra defects, foreign materials, count mix-ups, and other fabric abnormalities as they occur. The system generates detailed doff-, shift-, and day-wise reports.

The system supports ROI analysis to measure performance and cost savings, and seamlessly integrates with MES for centralized monitoring, traceability, and production optimization. Our advanced AI ensures continuous learning and adaptation to your specific production patterns.
