Knitting Inspection System

AI-based real-time inspection for knitting fabric quality

Overview

Real-Time Knitting Quality Monitoring

AI-based real-time inspection for knitting machines that continuously monitors fabric quality during production. The system accurately detects loop defects, miss-knits, slack loops, needle breaks, Lycra-out defects, and material contamination.

It supports automatic machine-stop integration to prevent defect propagation and ensure consistent quality. This automated approach eliminates human dependency while maintaining the highest standards of fabric excellence.
